About the Product

A single emergency companion before, during, and after a crisis.

The product video explains how ReadyDorm helps residents prepare information, trigger alerts, follow evacuation guidance, and keep emergency contacts informed without switching between different apps.

Transcript: ReadyDorm helps residents prepare an emergency profile, save trusted contacts, review routes, check a 72-hour backpack, and use SOS or safe check-ins during a crisis. Managers can monitor building status and coordinate response.

About the Team

Designed by students for students, roommates, families, and residence managers.

Our team focused on a clear, inclusive experience for people under pressure: simple language, responsive layouts, keyboard-friendly controls, and safety flows that are easy to scan.

Transcript: The ReadyDorm team researched residential emergency needs, designed user flows for preparation and response, and built a responsive landing page deployed through GitHub Pages as an academic prototype.

Core experience

ReadyDorm supports the people who need to act together.

Each role sees the information they need, from residents preparing a plan to managers coordinating an entire building.

SOS

Residents

Create an emergency profile, save contacts, review routes, check supplies, and trigger SOS or safe check-ins.

MAP

Emergency contacts

Receive alerts, view shared location, read critical medical details, and know whether the resident is safe.

OPS

Residence managers

Monitor resident status, send sector alerts, manage drills, and review equipment or incident reports.

Inclusion and accessibility

Built to be usable on different devices and under stressful conditions.

ReadyDorm uses English content, semantic HTML, visible focus states, responsive grids, alt text, captions, transcripts, keyboard-accessible controls, and reduced-motion support.

  • English interface with `lang="en"` for internationalization.
  • Responsive layout for mobile, tablet, and desktop.
  • Captions and transcripts for both required videos.
  • Accessible navigation labels and keyboard focus styles.
  • Clear contrast and plain language for emergency contexts.
