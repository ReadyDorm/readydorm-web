Emergency protocols

Clear action guides for the most common dorm emergencies.

ReadyDorm turns preparation into concrete steps residents can understand quickly, even from a phone during a stressful moment.

Earthquake

Protect yourself first

Drop, cover, hold on, avoid elevators, then follow the assigned evacuation route when movement is safe.

Fire

Evacuate with low visibility

Check door heat, stay low under smoke, use stairs, and notify contacts or managers after reaching the meeting point.

Gas leak

Reduce ignition risk

Avoid switches, open ventilation only if safe, leave the building, and report the incident from outside.

Flood

Move to safer zones

Keep away from contaminated water, protect essentials, and use the app to report damage and resident status.

Power outage

Stay visible and connected

Use flashlight mode, conserve battery, follow manager alerts, and confirm safety with roommates.

Medical event

Share critical details

Emergency contacts can access allergies, conditions, blood type, and instructions approved by the resident.

Manager tools

Residence staff can coordinate alerts, equipment, and reports from one place.

ReadyDorm includes a manager view for mass alerts, resident status, sector monitoring, brigadier tasks, equipment checks, drill history, and incident reports.

  • Send building-wide or sector-specific emergency messages.
  • Track who is safe, pending, or requesting help.
  • Review extinguishers, hoses, first-aid kits, and maintenance tasks.
  • Export drill and incident summaries for continuous improvement.
Inclusion and accessibility

Built to be usable on different devices and under stressful conditions.

ReadyDorm uses English content, semantic HTML, visible focus states, responsive grids, alt text, captions, transcripts, keyboard-accessible controls, and reduced-motion support.

  • English interface with `lang="en"` for internationalization.
  • Responsive layout for mobile, tablet, and desktop.
  • Captions and transcripts for both required videos.
  • Accessible navigation labels and keyboard focus styles.
  • Clear contrast and plain language for emergency contexts.
