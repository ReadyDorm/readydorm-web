Resident experience

Preparation and response screens for students and roommates.

Residents can prepare their data before an emergency and act quickly when something happens.

01

Emergency profile

Blood type, allergies, medication, accessibility needs, room number, preferred language, and instructions for responders.

02

Trusted contacts

Family, roommates, and emergency contacts receive safety status and location when the resident allows it.

03

72-hour backpack

Checklist for water, food, documents, medication, flashlight, charger, hygiene items, whistle, and cash.

04

Evacuation routes

Primary and alternate exits, floor maps, meeting points, and route notes that stay readable on mobile.

05

SOS and check-ins

Hold-to-send SOS, safe check-in, false alarm cancellation, flashlight support, and clear next steps.

06

Training and badges

Short lessons, drill participation, preparedness score, and badges that motivate repeated safety practice.

Residence operations

A complete dashboard for building-level coordination.

Managers can monitor resident status, send targeted alerts, check emergency equipment, coordinate brigadiers, and review drill reports.

  • Resident board grouped by safe, pending, and help requested.
  • Mass alerts by floor, sector, or full residence.
  • Equipment inventory for extinguishers, first-aid kits, hoses, and alarms.
  • Exportable drill and incident reports for continuous improvement.

Additional IoT feature

Smart sensor alerts make emergencies visible earlier.

The IoT feature connects dorm sensors to ReadyDorm so residents and managers can detect risk before it becomes harder to control.

Smoke sensor

Detects smoke and triggers a fire protocol alert with nearest exit guidance.

Gas sensor

Warns residents not to use switches and tells managers which sector needs evacuation.

Occupancy signal

Helps managers identify rooms that may still need check-in during evacuation.
